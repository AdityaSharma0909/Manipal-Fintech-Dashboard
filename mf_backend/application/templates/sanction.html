4.2 Classification of Loan Account as Special Mention Account (SMA) And Non-Performing Account (NPA)

Upon the occurrence of any payment default under this Loan, we shall classify Your loanaccount held with the Us (Loan Account) in accordance with the prevailing RBI norms and guidelines.

a) As per the current prevailing guidelines of RBI, Loan Account shall be Classified as Special Mention Account (SMA) on the following basis.



SMA Sub - Categories Basis of Classification Principal or interest Payment
Any other amount wholly or Partly due.
SMA-0 Up to 30 days
SMA-1 More than 30 days and up to 60 days
SMA-2 More than 60 days and up to 90 days


b) Further, the Loan Account shall be classified as Non-Performing Asset (NPA) where interest and/or instalment of principal remains overdue for a period of morethan 90 days in respect of a term loan;

c) Loan accounts classified as NPAs may be upgraded as 'standard' assets only if entire arrears of interest and principal Loan are paid by the Borrower

Examples of SMA/NPA classification dates:

If the due date of a loan account is March 31, 2021, and full dues are not received before the Lender runs the day-end process for this date, the date of overdue shall be March 31, 2021. If it continues to remain overdue, then this account shall get tagged as SMA-1 uponrunning the day-end process on April 30, 2021 i.e. upon completion of 30 days of being continuously overdue. Accordingly, the date of SMA-1 classification for that account shall be April 30, 2021. Similarly, if the account continues to remain overdue, it shall get tagged as SMA-2 upon running day-end process on May 30, 2021 and if continues to remain overdue further, it shall get classified as NPA upon running day-end process on June 29, 2021.
